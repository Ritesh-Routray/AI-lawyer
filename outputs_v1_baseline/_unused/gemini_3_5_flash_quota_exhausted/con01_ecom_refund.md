## Where you stand

You are a consumer who paid for a product that was delivered damaged and picked up for return [KB:cpa_consumer_def]. Failing to issue your refund of Rs 32,999 within the promised timeline amounts to a deficiency in service [KB:cpa_consumer_def]. You have strong proof of purchase and pickup via your order invoice and pickup receipt. Under consumer protection rules, you have 2 years from the date of the issue to take formal legal action [KB:cpa_limitation].

## Options

1. **Escalate to the E-Commerce Grievance Officer** (1 to 3 days to send; officer must acknowledge in 48 hours [KB:ecom_grievance_officer], Free): Find the contact details of the platform's designated Grievance Officer on their website or app. Send a written email with your order invoice, pickup receipt, and timeline, demanding a full refund within 7 days.

2. **File a complaint with National Consumer Helpline [KB:forum_nch] (NCH)** (15 to 30 days for resolution, Free): Call 1915 or lodge a grievance online at consumerhelpline.gov.in against the e-commerce platform attaching your invoice and pickup receipt.

3. **File a consumer complaint on e-Jagriti [KB:forum_ejagriti] portal** (3 to 6 months, Free (Nil court fee for claims under Rs 5 lakh) [KB:cpa_fee_nil_5lakh]): If the grievance officer and NCH do not resolve the issue, file a formal consumer complaint online against the company through the e-Jagriti portal [KB:forum_ejagriti] before the District Consumer Commission [KB:cpa_jurisdiction_2021].
